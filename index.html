        Projected categories ▸

        Pick whole categories or single subcategories. Every ledger line filed under one of them is rolled forward exactly one month at the same amount — its date plus a month, the day clamped so the 31st still lands in a short one. A projection holds only while its day is still ahead; the morning it passes it drops off, and the next statement rolls a fresher one into its place. Projections show as dashed lines and hollow pips, and never touch a balance.
